No song yet. A song is a list of patterns; each plays up to four of your sounds at once, for as long as the longest of them. Add one with + Pattern.

Patterns play in order. "Loop back" returns to the last "loop start"; "stop" ends the song there. Start it in your cart with music(0); music(-1) stops it.

Luanette playground

A tiny Lua game console for ESP32-S3 boards, running here on the same engine compiled to WebAssembly.

Playing

  • A tap or click is A, a drag is the d-pad.
  • Keys: arrows, Z (or Space) for A, X for B, Shift+arrows to tilt.
  • A gamepad works too: the d-pad or left stick, the bottom and right buttons, the right stick tilts.

Editing

  • Ctrl+Enter runs your edits; Ctrl+Space lists the API; hover a name for its help.
  • Ctrl+F finds, Ctrl+H replaces.
  • In Sprites, Sounds and Music: Ctrl+Z undoes, Ctrl+C / V copy and paste; Space plays a sound.

Your carts

Edits and your own carts are kept in this browser. Save as .lua keeps a copy anywhere else. A share link holds the whole cart, its sounds and song too: nothing is uploaded, the link is the only copy. Send to the board puts the cart on a board plugged in over USB, under its name; an edited example replaces the built-in one until you restore it.
